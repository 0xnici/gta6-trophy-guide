Missable trophies — flagged from the official descriptions

A trophy is flagged here only where its official description points at a one-time window: a unique target, a single scripted moment, a choice, or a first attempt that cannot be replayed the same way. Everything else on the list can be cleaned up in free roam after the story. Each card below says why it is flagged; the full guide on the card cites its sources. As a general precaution, finish the stranger strands before the finale — strand-givers' stories end with the campaign.

Export, import, reset

Your checklist lives only in this browser (localStorage). Export it before you clear site data or change machines — an export is the only backup. Import accepts a file this guide exported and restores it exactly: earned trophies, counters and settings.
